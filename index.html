About Us

Owner Harsh Soni

What began in a small workshop in the heart of the jewelry district has blossomed into a celebrated house known for impeccable craftsmanship.

Today, our third-generation artisans continue the tradition, blending time-honored techniques with contemporary design sensibilities. Every piece that leaves our workshop carries the weight of this legacy.

The Art of Creation

Crafted by Hand, Treasured Forever

Each Soni Jewellers piece undergoes a meticulous journey from concept to completion. Our artisans employ traditional goldsmithing techniques passed down through generations, ensuring every curve, texture, and finish meets our exacting standards.

  • Hand-selected materials from ethical sources
  • Traditional lost-wax casting and hand-forging
  • Multiple quality inspections at every stage
  • Personalized finishing and custom sizing
